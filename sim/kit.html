Low-Data Kit

Save this page on your phone before you need it. It works with no connection: it turns the codes in text messages into pictures and maps, checks that alerts are genuine, and makes codes to send a photo or your location by plain SMS. Draw a box on a picture (or tap a numbered part) to ask for a closer look; a route opens in an offline map app.

Send a photo by SMS

Shrunk to 128 pixels (usually 3–12 SMS). The service describes it and answers questions about it.
